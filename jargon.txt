Caterkiller jargon guide

Signal
A signal is the bot's current opinion about what the market is doing. In Caterkiller, a signal is generated by evaluateSignal(). The bot looks at the latest price, recent price history, and whatever strategy is active, then decides whether the next action should be:
- buy
- sell
- nothing (null)

A signal is just a rule-based trigger. It isn't magic; it's the program saying, "based on these numbers, I think the next trade should be X."

A strategy is basically the bot's trading personality or decision formula.

Quick version
- signal = a buy/sell/no-op decision
- strategy = the rule that creates that decision
- banner = the startup display text
- badnik = robot enemy / mascot vibe
- caterkiller = the bot and the whole trading theme

In short: the bot watches prices, picks a signal based on a strategy, prints a banner during startup, and pretends to be a tiny robot menace from a Sonic-like world.
